## About HeathLabs

**HeathLabs is an independent Australian product and engineering studio.**

We build software, intelligent systems, automotive technology and consumer products with a focus on usefulness, security, thoughtful design and real-world reliability.

Different products. Same philosophy:

**make complicated things useful, understandable and dependable.**

---

## What we're building

### Forge

**Engineering should be Understandable.**

An engineering operating system for knowledge, documentation, orchestration, tooling and AI-assisted development.

Built to become the common engineering layer beneath HeathLabs products and technical domains.

`Engineering knowledge` · `Automation` · `AI workflows` · `Documentation` · `falconOS`

---

### Atlas

**Travel should feel like an experience, not an administration task.**

A human-centred travel platform combining planning, bookings, maps, memories, recommendations and shared experiences into one coherent workspace.

`Travel planning` · `Bookings` · `Maps` · `Memories` · `Social travel` · `Mobile`

---

### KinStream

**Context that grows with you.**

A persistent-context companion platform built around continuity, shared experiences, projects, media, ideas and memories.

The goal is not another chatbot, but an intelligent system capable of understanding how the things people share connect over time.

`Persistent context` · `Multimodal` · `Privacy` · `Shared experiences` · `Mobile + desktop`

---

### Lift

**People helping people — with trust built in.**

A trust-first community platform exploring a safer way for people to help others reduce legitimate financial debt.

Verification, independent confirmation and scalable review are designed into the platform from the beginning.

`Verification` · `Trust infrastructure` · `Privacy` · `Fraud resistance` · `Community support`

---

### HeathenGarage

**Engineering for people who actually care about cars.**

The automotive engineering and product arm of HeathLabs.

Focused on calibration, electronics, vehicle systems, software, hardware and enthusiast-led product development.

`Calibration` · `Vehicle electronics` · `Automotive software` · `Embedded hardware` · `Performance engineering`

---

### HeathLabs Control

**The system behind the systems.**

The internal operational layer keeping HeathLabs projects, decisions, documentation and development workflows aligned.

## How we build

### Human first
Technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.

### Security and privacy by default
Identity, financial data, personal context and infrastructure deserve strong boundaries from day one.

### Documentation is infrastructure
Important decisions and knowledge should survive beyond the person or conversation that created them.

### Build for the real world
Products should handle imperfect data, unreliable networks, user mistakes, recovery paths and long-term maintenance.

### Cross-platform from the beginning
Web, Android, iOS and desktop futures should be considered at the architectural stage where appropriate.

### AI should multiply good engineering
AI accelerates engineering. It does not replace architecture, judgement or responsibility.

### Understandability matters
Complexity may be unavoidable. Confusion should not be.

### Quality compounds
We prefer foundations that remain useful for years over shortcuts that create tomorrow's problems.
